BIO 93 — DNA to Organisms
Instructor: Dr. Elena Vasquez (office hours Tue/Thu 2-3pm)
Fall 2026 · MWF 10:00-10:50am

COURSE DESCRIPTION
An introduction to molecular biology, genetics, and cell biology, building from the
structure of DNA through gene expression to the organization of multicellular organisms.

GRADING
- Midterm 1: 20%
- Midterm 2: 20%
- Final Exam: 35%

- Participation: 10%

TOPICS BY WEEK
Week 1: DNA structure and the central dogma
Week 2: DNA replication and repair mechanisms
Week 3: Transcription and RNA processing
Week 4: Translation and the genetic code
Week 5: Regulation of gene expression in prokaryotes
Week 6: Regulation of gene expression in eukaryotes
Week 7: Mendelian genetics and inheritance patterns
Week 8: Linkage, recombination, and genetic mapping
Week 9: Mutations and DNA damage
Week 10: Cell cycle and mitosis
Week 11: Meiosis and sexual reproduction
Week 12: Developmental biology and differentiation
